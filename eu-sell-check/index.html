01One EU rep per country

From 12 Aug 2026, a seller shipping packaged goods direct to end users in an EU country where it is not established needs an EPR authorised representative there, by written mandate. No small-volume exemption.1

02Marketplaces check first

Online platforms must get your EPR registration before letting you sell, and German marketplaces may only list you if you are in LUCID and a packaging system.1,3

03GPSR on every listing

Since 13 Dec 2024 a non-EU maker needs an EU-based responsible person, named with postal and email address on the listing.5,6

04Germany: up to €200,000

Up to €100,000 for no LUCID registration, up to €200,000 for no packaging system, and a sales ban.2,3
