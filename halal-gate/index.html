Deadline, label, certificate route, filer and penalties.

01Deadlines moved to 2028

GR 40/2026 (14 Oct 2026) moved imported food and drink to 18 Oct 2028, and cosmetics, chemicals and listed consumer goods to 17 Oct 2028.

02150 foreign bodies listed

SIHALAL lists 150 foreign halal bodies in 45 countries, 114 with an active MRA (15 Jun 2026). Recognition is per product category.

03Non-halal label rule

BPJPH Reg. 3/2026: non-halal products carry a non-halal mark. Products already on sale have until 23 Jul 2027.

04Per-shipment check repealed

The pre-shipment Halal Product Assurance report (Reg. 4/2026, in force 9 Sep 2026) was repealed with GR 40/2026.
