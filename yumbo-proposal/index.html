How it is built, and why that matters to you

You are not buying a website with a login on it. The parts you cannot see are the parts that decide whether you can still trust it in three years:

  • Every screen is opened and clicked by a machine before it reaches you — every button, every row in every list, on a desktop and on a phone. If something is clickable and does nothing, the build fails and does not ship.
  • Your records are checked for contradictions on every single run. That is how the six problems above were found — not by anyone noticing, but automatically.
  • Every change to your database is a numbered, repeatable step — sixteen so far — rehearsed on a copy of your data before it ever touches the real thing.
  • Backups are proven by restoring them, not by trusting that they ran.
  • Money is calculated in exactly one place in the code. Job profit, commission, subcontractor payouts and your balances all come from the same calculation, so two screens can never quietly disagree about what you are owed.
  • Nobody sees a figure they should not. Access is checked at the screen and at each individual number — hiding a page is not the same as hiding the money on it.

Built and maintained by one person who holds a bachelor's degree in computer programming — which is why you talk to the person who writes the code, rather than to an account manager.
