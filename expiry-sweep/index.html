Your Amazon deductions have an expiry date

Since 2025, Amazon gives 1P shortage and price disputes a hard limit of two years from the invoice date.1 Every month, another month of invoices passes that line and the money is gone for good. We find the invalid ones before they expire, and we are paid only from money that comes back.

What changed

Two years, hard-coded

Shortage (PQV) and price (PPV) disputes older than two years from the invoice date are rejected by Vendor Central. Before 2025 the window was longer in some cases.1

One line at a time

Bulk shortage claims are gone. Each shortage line is now filed on its own, and re-filings link back to the first case.2

Evidence decides

Disputes are judged on the documents behind them: the BOL, proof of delivery and the invoice. Generic disputes are less and less likely to win.3

Questions

Is my data safe?

For the free report we only need one export file. We do not ask for a login, and we never accept a shared password.

If you sign, your own admin creates a named user for us with the rights you choose, and you can remove it at any time. The agreement limits what we may do with your data, and we delete it when the work ends. If you do not go ahead after the report, tell us and we delete your file.

Which retailers do you cover?

Amazon Vendor Central (1P) first: shortages, then price claims, then chargebacks and co-op. After that, Walmart (APDP), Target, Kroger and Home Depot. Each retailer has its own window and evidence rules, and we work to each one.

This is for suppliers who sell to the retailer. It is not Seller Central (3P) reimbursement.

What do you need from me?

For the free report: your Vendor Central deductions or remittance export, or your AR deduction report.

After you sign: a named user in the retailer portal, your payment advice files (often EDI 820 and 812 from your EDI provider), and proof-of-delivery documents (BOL, POD, carrier tracking) from your 3PL or carrier.

Who files the disputes?

Our trained team files through the named user you create. Where a retailer does not allow that, we prepare each packet and your staff clicks submit. We do not use bots in retailer portals.

Do you work with every supplier?

Today we work with suppliers selling roughly $5M to $75M a year through these retailers. For now we are not taking on suppliers based in Florida, Washington, Illinois, North Carolina or North Dakota.
