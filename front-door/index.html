Ask for my demo

Questions

Will callers know it is an AI?

Yes, on every call. The first sentence says it is your company's AI assistant and that the call is recorded. If a caller does not want to be recorded, it takes a message instead. We never pretend it is a person.

Do I need a new number or app?

No. You keep your number. You turn on call forwarding for busy, no-answer and after-hours calls, usually a short code or a setting with your phone company. If your carrier does not allow that, we help you use a second published number for after-hours calls and web enquiries.

What does it say about prices or my services?

Only what you have written down for us: your services, area, hours and any prices you publish. If you change something, text or email us one line and we update it.

Does it send text messages?

At launch it works by voice and email. Text replies come later, once carrier registration is approved for your business. It never sends marketing texts to anyone.

Who is it for?

Trade and home-service businesses: HVAC, plumbing, roofing, garage doors, electrical and similar. We are not taking law, medical or dental offices yet.

What happens to the recordings?

You get a link to each one. We keep recordings for 90 days and then delete them. Your business stays the owner of its customer data.
