Your feedback is in. Here is what changed.

Alex — you said it was too complicated, that you could not type into anything, and that you kept losing your place. All three are fixed. This page shows you what is different and how to use it. Five minutes.

Open the preview Nothing you do here is saved — try anything, reload to start over

Open it on your phone as well as your computer. It is built for both, and most of you will use it standing on a job site.

1You can type into every number now

This was the big one. Before, the pricing numbers were just printed on the screen — the page even said you could change them, and you could not.

Now every box is a box you type in. Profit %, labor rate, sales tax, GC's %, contingency. Type 38 and you get 38%. Type 32 and the labor rate is $32 an hour. The totals update while you type — there is no save button between you and the number.

The job-type dropdown is gone

It used to force you to pick "Welding / metal" and take whatever profit and labor rate came with it. Now those presets are just a shortcut — tap one to fill in the boxes, then type over anything you want. The boxes are in charge. The preset is only a head start.

2Line items are real rows

Quantity, materials, labor hours, sub cost, equipment — all typed. Add a row with + Add line item, remove one with the × on its right. The grey boxes beside them are the results (materials total, tax, labor, cost, profit) — those are worked out for you, so they are not typeable on purpose.

There is a pick from price list button on each row, and if the property has step measurements on record they show at the top of the costing section. You no longer have to leave the screen to look either up.

3One screen per estimate, not four

It used to be four separate pages with nothing joining them. Now it is one screen with tabs down the top — Details · Costing · Quote document · History — and a bar at the bottom that follows you with the running total and the Save and Submit buttons.

4You always know where you are

There is a trail at the top of every screen — Home › Quotes › 26-4611 — and every part of it is tappable. No more going back to the tiles to get anywhere.

Your tiles are down to the five you actually use: Home, New work, Quotes, Jobs, Requests. Price list and step sizes moved inside the costing screen where you need them, so they are no longer separate places to visit.

5After you submit, you can still ask

Once you submit an estimate it locks — that is on purpose, so a price cannot quietly change after somebody has approved it. But there is now a Request a change button on it.

You say what needs changing. Mailin or Daniela see it, and they can unlock it for you — just you, not everybody. You make the change, submit again, and it locks again. Every version is kept, so nobody has to remember what the old price was.

6Your day starts on Home

Quotes needing work, requests pending, and anything sent in from the field — with the oldest thing that needs you at the top.

What we need from you

Please open it and actually try to price a job — type real numbers into 26-4611 and see whether it behaves the way you would expect. That is worth more to us than a read-through.

Then tell us:

  • Anything you tried to tap that did not do what you expected
  • Any number you wanted to change and could not
  • Anything on the screen you would not use, so we can take it off
  • Anything missing that you use every day

Say it plainly — "this is annoying" is useful. It is much cheaper to change now than after it is built.

Two things to know while you are looking

  • Nothing is saved. Type anything you like. Reload the page and it goes back to the start.
  • The names and properties are yours, the numbers are examples. We used real property and sub names so it feels like your work. Phone numbers and emails are deliberately left out.
