Chapter 1

Getting around

Sign in, find your way, and see what needs you today.

Sign in

Sign in

  1. Open yumbo.noumansadiq.com on your computer or phone.
  2. Type your email and password. Press Sign in.
On your phone: after you sign in, use "Add to Home Screen" in your browser. It then opens like an app.

Want a new password? Tap Change password at the bottom of the menu.

The menu

The menu, in three groups

Work is everything about jobs and quotes. People is your subs, suppliers and team. Money is who owes you, who you owe, and your company numbers.

A red number on a button means something is waiting there.

The search box at the top finds any job, client, quote or invoice. Type a name or a job number.

Too busy? The ☰ button hides the menu. The ⚙ button opens Settings.
Home

Home: what needs you today

Start every day here. Three boxes at the top:

  1. Owed to you: money your clients still have to pay.
  2. You owe subs: what you still have to pay your subs.
  3. Kept this month: your share after subs and costs.

Under Needs you, every card is something waiting for your yes: quotes, requests, invoices, late payments, and new work nobody has quoted. Tap the underlined words to go straight there.

Chapter 3

New work: nothing gets forgotten

Every time a property calls or emails asking for work, write it here. It stays on the list until somebody quotes it.

Work › New work

Write down a new request

  1. Go to Work › New work, or press + Log a bid on Home.
  2. Type the property, who asked, their phone or email, and what they need.
  3. Press + Add this work.
The 4-day rule. Anything nobody touches for 4 days turns red on your Home screen, so no request sits forgotten.

The age badge (2d, 7d) shows how long each one has waited. Tabs at the top show the ones with a meeting set, the ones already quoted, and the ones you dropped.

Open one to move it forward

Tap a request. Pick a date and press Log site-visit meeting, press Ready, build estimate to price it, or Drop this bid if the client changed their mind. Dropped ones are kept, never deleted.

Chapter 4

Quotes, priced the way you price

Labour, materials and the sub's price, then your profit and contingency on top. The total to the client works itself out as you type.

Work › Quotes

Make a quote

  1. Press + New estimate on Home or on Quotes.
  2. Type what the work is, pick the client and the estimator.
  3. Type labour, materials and the sub's price.
  4. Check profit % and contingency %. They start from your own defaults.
  5. Add up to 8 site photos if you have them. Press Create estimate.
The tick box. Leave "The sub's price already covers the materials" ticked for a normal job. Untick it only when you buy the materials yourself.

The quotes list

See every quote waiting, approved or lost, and your close rate at the top.

Press Remind each time you follow up with a client. The count shows how many times you have chased.

Your yes

Say yes, and it becomes a job

  1. Press Review & decide on a quote.
  2. Check the costs and what the client pays.
  3. Press Approve, create the job, or Mark lost.
The client sees one number. Your costs and percentages stay inside. Only the total goes on what you send them.

Lost quotes are kept, never deleted.

Work › Call back

Call back: clients who said no

When a client turns a quote down, the system brings them back to ring again after 14 days, with what they said last time and the number you quoted.

Nothing is sent to them by itself. It is a list for you or your secretary to call.

Chapter 6

Paying subs, without remembering a thing

You pay a sub to start, you ask the property for money, and at the end you pay the sub the rest. The system keeps track of every dollar, for every sub, on every job.

1Add the sub to the jobType what you agreed, once
2Pay his start moneyPress Pay
3Invoice the propertyChapter 7
4Property pays youPress Record
5Pay him the restThe balance is already there
Step 1 · on the job page

Add the sub to the job

  1. Open the job. Scroll to Subcontractors.
  2. Pick the sub and type the amount you agreed with him.
  3. Press Add sub. Need two subs on one job? Add another the same way.
From now on the job shows three numbers for him: Agreed payout, Paid so far, and Balance.
Steps 2 and 5 · Money › You owe

Pay a sub: deposit, part, or the rest

  1. Go to Money › You owe.
  2. Find the sub on that job. Type the amount you are paying him, and a note if you want ("Zelle", "check 1042").
  3. Press Pay.
Paying in three or four parts? Press Pay each time. The system adds them up. When his balance reaches zero, he drops off this list.

This list is your answer to "who do I still owe?" Every sub, every job, one screen. The orange "40d" badge means the job finished 40 days ago and he is still waiting.

People › Subcontractors

Everything about one sub

Open a sub to see what you agreed with him on each job, what you paid, what you still owe, and every payment with its date and note.

If he calls asking for money, open his page. The answer is right there.
Your old sheet

"From your sheet, please confirm"

Your old sheet said what you agreed with each sub, but not what you paid. Those jobs wait at the bottom of You owe for one tap each:

  1. Already paid: nothing owed.
  2. Still owed: it moves into your list above.
  3. Not a sub cost: it was never a sub payment.

Until you answer, these are not counted in what you owe, so your totals never include a guess.

Chapter 7

Invoices that can't go out by accident

Ask a property for money, check it, send it, and record it when they pay.

On the job page

Make an invoice

  1. Open the job. Scroll to Invoices for this job and press + New invoice.
  2. Type the invoice number and the amount. For a deposit, type just the deposit amount.
  3. Press Create invoice.
Nothing is sent yet. A new invoice waits for your approval first.
Money › Invoice approvals

Check it and send it

  1. Go to Money › Invoice approvals.
  2. Press Preview to see exactly what the client will get.
  3. Pick how you are sending it (email, check, bank transfer, their portal).
  4. Press Approve & mark sent.
Alex can prepare invoices. You still have the final say before anything reaches a client.

What the client receives

A clean invoice with your company name, the job, the total and the balance due. From the preview you can print it or save it as a PDF to email.

Money › Who owes you

When a property pays you

  1. Go to Money › Who owes you.
  2. Find the invoice. Type the amount that came in, and the check number in the note.
  3. Press Record.
Part payments work. The invoice stays on the list until it is paid in full. When the final invoice on a job is paid in full, the job closes by itself.

The boxes at the top split what you are owed by how late it is: 0 to 30 days, 31 to 60, and over 60.

Chapter 8

Spending, always against the right job

Materials, fuel, dump fees and requests from the field all land on the job they were for. So each job's profit is real.

On the job page

Materials and receipts

On the job, under Materials / receipts, type the date, supplier, cost and a note. Press Add.

Below it, Transaction history lists every payment on the job: money in from the client and money out to subs.

On the job page

Spent on site: fuel, dump fees, tool hire

  1. On the job, press Log something spent.
  2. Type what it was, the amount, who paid, and how.
  3. It waits for Approve before it counts against the job.
Paid with their own card? It shows "owed back", so you know who to pay back.
Work › Requests

Requests from the field

Your team asks here instead of calling you: more materials, a change order the client wants, an incident on site.

  1. Open a request from Work › Requests or from Home.
  2. Write a note if you want. It goes back to whoever asked.
  3. Press Approve or Decline.

Approving does not send any money. You still pay from your bank or Zelle as always. The system keeps the record.

Making a request

Anyone on your team presses + New request, picks the type and the job, and writes what they need.

Chapter 9

Your team: everyone sees only what you allow

Add your people, decide what each person can see, and get answers without chasing.

People › Settings

Add a person

  1. Go to Settings › People and scroll to Add someone.
  2. Type their name and email (their login), pick their post (secretary, estimator, project manager, sales).
  3. The post ticks the usual boxes. Change any tick for this one person.
  4. Save. A one-time password appears on the next screen. Give it to them. They choose their own the first time they sign in.
Money stays yours. Without "See money figures", a person sees the work but not the dollar amounts. Switch anyone off at any time. What they did is kept.

Work › Updates

Ask for an update, and let the system chase

  1. On any job, press Ask someone for an update.
  2. Pick who, what about, how they should answer, and how soon. Type your question.
  3. They answer in the system. Anything not answered in time moves to Late on your Updates page, so nothing is forgotten.
Everyone can answer. Whoever you ask sees the question waiting for them, even if they can't open anything else.
Settings › Commission

Commission, agreed upfront

Set a rate for everyone, for a post, or for one person. When a job is assigned, the person sees what they will earn and agrees to it. After that it can't change for that job without their yes.

Old rates are kept, so a commission paid in August still makes sense in November.

Settings › Preferences

Your rules, in plain words

Deposit percentage, the job size that needs a deposit, your default profit, contingency, labour rate and sales tax, and when to call back a client who said no. Change them any time.

Alerts

Alerts

The bell at the top. Each person sees only their own, like a request waiting for you or an answer that came back. Tap one to go straight to it.

Chapter 10

Your company, in one number

Not just each job. The whole business: what came in, what went out, and whether it is safe to pay yourself.

Money › Company

The owner pay check

The top line answers one question: is it safe to pay yourself today? Under it: invoiced this year, profit from jobs this month, payroll, monthly overhead, and what the company kept this month.

Early in a month it often says "Hold off", because rent and payroll come before job money lands. It turns green as invoices get paid.

Jobs earning under 30% show in a watchlist underneath.

Overhead, payroll and company expenses

From Company, open Overhead for monthly fixed costs (rent, insurance, truck lease), Payroll for what you paid your staff, and Company expenses for spending that isn't for one job.

Type each one once. Overhead counts every month until you give it an end date.

Money › Reports

Reports

Jobs won and lost, your close rate, what you are owed and what you owe, month by month what you invoiced and kept, and each estimator's record.

Chapter 11

Subs, suppliers and clients

Type each one once. Every job, quote and payment links back to them.

People › Subcontractors

Add a sub, with his 1099 details

  1. Go to People › Subcontractors.
  2. Type his legal name, trade, language and phone. Only the name is required.
  3. If you have them: tax ID, address, and tick "I already have their signed W-9".
  4. Press Add subcontractor.
Ready for January. Once you pay a sub over $2,000 in a year, the system reminds you if his 1099 details are missing. Never type a bank account number. Only a Zelle name or last four digits.
Chapter 12

Keeping your records clean

Money › Needs a look

Things that look wrong, for you to decide

The system spots records that look doubled ("Rivera Drywall" and "Rivera Drywall LLC") or that don't add up. Each one is a question for you.

  1. Merge into one if they are the same.
  2. Rename if the name is wrong.
  3. Leave them if they really are different.

Nothing changes until you answer. Answer once and it won't ask again.

Chapter 13

Connections

Link your own accounts when you are ready. Nothing starts running the moment you connect. You switch on each feature yourself.

People › Connections

Telegram and Google

  1. Telegram: your crew sends a photo with the job's code (it is on every job page, like 4312-BV) and it lands on that job. Photos without a code wait in Work › Team inbox for someone to confirm.
  2. Google: a folder for each job in your Drive, and invoices sent from your own email. We switch this on with you when you are ready.

Google can only see files the system made. It can't read the rest of your Drive or your inbox.

Work › Team inbox

Team inbox

Photos and messages from the field, sorted by what they are (a payment, a job update, new work). Pick the job and press Confirm & link, or Dismiss.

Quick answers

You might be wondering

I typed the wrong amount.

Send me the job number and what it should be. I'll correct it the same day.

Who can see the money pages?

Only you, unless you tick "See money figures" or a money tab for someone in Settings.

Can Alex make quotes and invoices?

Yes. You still approve every quote before it becomes a job, and every invoice before it goes out.

Is my data safe?

A full copy is made every night. Your data is yours: ask any time and you get all of it.

Does approving something send money?

No. Nothing in the system moves money. You pay the way you always do. The system keeps the record.

Where do I start?

With your subs. Add each one to their jobs, record what you've paid, and you'll always know what's left.
