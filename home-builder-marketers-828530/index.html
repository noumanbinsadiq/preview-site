How this is worked out

Each paying customer is credited once, to the first lead they came in as on or before their first invoice — matched by email, then phone, then exact name.

Close-but-not-exact matches are listed for a person to confirm and are never counted. Profit is shown only where the job has costs recorded; it is never assumed.
