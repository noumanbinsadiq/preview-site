Rates checked 26 Nov 2026. Duty is a percent of the customs value of the goods (roughly the factory price). Freight, insurance, the 0.3464% merchandise processing fee and broker fees are left out because they are about the same from either country. IEEPA "reciprocal" and fentanyl duties stopped on 24 Feb 2026 after the Supreme Court ruling, and the 10% Section 122 duty that replaced them ended on 24 Jul 2026, so both show as 0% for China and Pakistan (the earlier ~19% US-Pakistan reciprocal rate is no longer collected). Your exact code depends on material and make-up: synthetic (PU) gloves and polyester uniforms classify differently, so verify with your broker. MOQ and sample times are typical ranges, not quotes.
